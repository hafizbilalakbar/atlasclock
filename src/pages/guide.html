Time Zone Fundamentals

Coordinated Universal Time (UTC)

The primary time standard by which the world regulates clocks and time. All time zones are defined by their offset from UTC, ranging from UTC-12 to UTC+14.

UTC Offset System

Each time zone has a fixed offset from UTC, typically in whole hours but some regions use half-hour (UTC+5:30 India) or quarter-hour offsets (UTC+5:45 Nepal).

Daylight Saving Time (DST)

Many regions adjust their clocks forward by one hour during summer months to extend evening daylight. Not all countries observe DST.

International Date Line

An imaginary line on the Earth's surface at roughly 180° longitude where the date changes by one day when crossed.
